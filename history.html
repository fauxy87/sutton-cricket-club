The story so far

Cricket in Sutton since 1877

Sutton Cricket Club records its foundation as 1877. That founding year remains part of the club badge and identity today.

We do not yet have a complete verified year-by-year account stretching back through every generation of the club. Rather than fill those gaps with assumptions, this digital history records what can be supported by club material and grows as more reliable records are found.
