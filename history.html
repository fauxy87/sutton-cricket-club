The beginning

Founded in 1877

Sutton Cricket Club records its foundation as 1877. The club badge, website identity and current records all carry that founding year.

At the moment, we do not have a complete verified year-by-year history stretching back to the nineteenth century. Rather than fill those gaps with assumptions, this page will grow as scorebooks, committee records, photographs, newspaper cuttings and other reliable club material are found.
