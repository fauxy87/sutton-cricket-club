Colin Coulson Memorial Game

A special day of cricket, remembrance and fundraising in Colin’s memory.

← Club news
Club community

A huge thank you to everyone who came along and supported the memorial game between the Coulson XI and Paul’s XI.

The Coulson XI scored 181/6, before Paul’s XI reached 187/5 to win by five wickets.

Batting highlights

🏏 Coulson XI — Lourens Herselman 32*, Phil Coulson 30* and Kaiden Grady 27

🏏 Paul’s XI — Paul Robinson 33*, David Frear 33* and Ricky Paul 33*

Best bowling figures

🎯 Paul’s XI — Jeff Paul 2/17

🎯 Coulson XI — Lourens Herselman 2/27 and Leon Perrin 2/38

The day was all about coming together to remember Colin and celebrate his life. Thanks to everyone’s incredible generosity, we raised £275 for the British Heart Foundation.

Thank you to all the players, helpers and supporters who made it such a special day in Colin’s memory.
