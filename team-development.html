Cricket for experience and enjoyment

What is the Sunday Development XI?

Sunday cricket provides an opportunity to enjoy match cricket, gain experience and develop confidence in a team environment. It's part of Sutton's wider pathway between junior and senior cricket.

Junior progression

For eligible younger players ready for the next step, development matches can provide valuable experience alongside more established cricketers. Participation depends on age, readiness and appropriate safeguarding arrangements.
