A place for every young cricketer

The Sutton junior cricket pathway

Whether your child is picking up a bat for the first time or wants more match experience, our aim is to make cricket welcoming, enjoyable and rewarding.

First stepsFun activities to build confidence, teamwork, throwing, catching and batting skills. Our 2027 beginner programme is still being finalised.
Developing skillsYoung players can build on the basics with coaching and age-appropriate cricket. Groups and session times will be confirmed before registration.
Matches & progressionJunior match opportunities and a pathway towards Sunday development and senior cricket, where appropriate for each player.
