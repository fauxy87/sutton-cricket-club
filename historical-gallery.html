Memories of the ground

The old pavilion

Among the photographs are views of the former cricket ground and pavilion, with St Andrew's Church in the background. Club recollections tell how former President Stan Darby donated a replacement pavilion after an earlier building was damaged by a roller. The present pavilion opened around 2000.

The exact dates are still being researched. See ground and pavilion photographs → · Read the pavilion history →
