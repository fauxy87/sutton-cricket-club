Vice Presidents

Historical archive. This list and role description were recovered from Sutton Cricket Club's former Pitchero site. No contact details are reproduced.

Vice Presidents support the continuation and future success of Sutton Cricket Club through goodwill and active support within the local and cricketing communities. Support can include attending matches, offering professional or other advice, helping with club tasks and supporting the club within the community.

Appointment as a Vice President also makes the individual an Honorary Member. New Vice Presidents are considered by the General Committee and subsequently confirmed at the club's AGM. The former club guidance states that appointments are automatically renewed unless the Vice President requests otherwise.
