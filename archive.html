Supporting Sutton Cricket Club

Vice Presidents support the continuation and future success of the club through goodwill and active involvement in the local and cricketing communities. Support can include attending matches, offering advice, helping with club tasks and promoting Sutton CC.

Appointment also makes a Vice President an Honorary Member. The historical club guidance states that new Vice Presidents are considered by the General Committee and confirmed at the AGM, with appointments renewed unless the Vice President requests otherwise.
