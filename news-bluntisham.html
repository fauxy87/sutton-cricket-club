Grady century powers Sutton to huge victory

Sutton beat Bluntisham by 201 runs.

← Club news
1st XI

Michael Grady hit 127* as Sutton made 288/3. James Nuttall scored 80 and Richard Goodjohn 50*. Bluntisham were dismissed for 87 as Sutton won by 201 runs.
