Policies & Safeguarding

Sutton Cricket Club is committed to providing a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment. Our current safeguarding and conduct policies were approved by the Club Committee on 17 September 2026.

Safeguarding

Concerned about someone's welfare?

Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility. If you have a concern about a child, young person or adult at risk involved with Sutton Cricket Club, please contact a Club Safeguarding Officer.
